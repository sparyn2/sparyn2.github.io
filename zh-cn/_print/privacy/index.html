Privacy Policy

Sparyn privacy policy for website visitors and B2B customers.

Sparyn respects your privacy. Information submitted through our contact form or email is used only to respond to eveningwear inquiries, prepare quotes, and fulfill orders. We do not sell personal data to third parties.

Designs, tech packs, and reference samples shared for quoting are treated as confidential. Access is limited to sales and production staff involved in your project.

We use basic analytics to improve site performance. You may request deletion of inquiry data by emailing sales@sparyn.com.
