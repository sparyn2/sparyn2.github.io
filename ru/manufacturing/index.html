From brief to export carton

Four transparent stages with clear deliverables at each gate.

1

Brief and Pattern

Tech pack review, measurement grading, sequin map, and fabric/lining confirmation within one business day of a complete brief.

2

Sampling

Proto or SMS in 7–12 days with fit notes, stretch tests, and photo approval before bulk deposit.

3

Bulk Production

Cutting, sequin application, sewing, and in-line QC with defect tracking and optional mid-line photo updates.

4

QC and Export

AQL 2.5 inspection, needle detection on request, packing list, and shipment documents for your destination.
